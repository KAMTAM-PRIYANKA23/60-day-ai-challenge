# ❤️ About LifeLink

LifeLink is an AI-powered emergency healthcare coordination platform that helps patients quickly connect with hospitals, blood banks, and verified blood donors during medical emergencies.

Instead of searching multiple services individually, patients can submit one emergency request while the system intelligently prioritizes the situation and recommends the most appropriate healthcare resources.
