# ❤️ LifeLink

**AI-Powered Emergency Healthcare Coordination Platform**

LifeLink is an AI-assisted emergency healthcare platform that helps patients quickly connect with nearby hospitals, blood banks, verified blood donors, and emergency resources during critical situations.

The goal is to reduce response time by intelligently prioritizing emergencies and recommending the most suitable healthcare resources based on urgency, availability, compatibility, and location.

# 🎯 Project Vision

LifeLink aims to make emergency healthcare coordination faster and more accessible by combining AI-assisted decision-making with real-time healthcare resource information.

Rather than searching multiple services during emergencies, patients can submit a single request and receive prioritized recommendations for hospitals, blood banks, and verified donors through one platform.
